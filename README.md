🩺 AI & Clinical Decision Support Systems: A 30-Day Research Journey



📌 Project Overview
This repository documents a 30-day accelerated research pipeline bridging foundational machine learning with advanced clinical deep learning architectures. The primary goal is to investigate how artificial intelligence can be safely and transparently integrated into clinical decision support systems (CDSS).

Core Research Objectives
This codebase serves as the technical foundation for exploring the following critical areas:
1. **Explainable AI (XAI) & Cognitive Bias:** Evaluating the role of XAI in reducing cognitive bias during clinical decision-making.
2. **Quantifying Trust:** Developing a mathematical framework for measuring clinician trust in XAI.
3. **Diagnostic Uncertainty:** Assessing the impact of XAI on reducing diagnostic uncertainty.
4. **Human-AI Interaction:** Identifying factors that influence healthcare professionals' trust in CDSS.
5. **Catastrophic Forgetting:** Researching how continual learning mechanisms can prevent model degradation over time, ensuring decision quality and patient safety.
